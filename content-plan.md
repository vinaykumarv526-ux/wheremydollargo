# WhereMyDollarGo: 30-Day Launch Content Plan & Master Blueprint

Channel Handle: **@WhereMyDollarGo**  
Tagline: *Every dollar, explained.*

---

## 1. Core Playlists & Structure

1. **Where Does $100 Go?** — Itemized economic dissections of everyday spending and deductions.
2. **Insurance in 60 Seconds** — Breaking down terms, claim processes, and policy fine print.
3. **Money Mechanics** — Core banking, credit, compound growth, and debt strategies.
4. **Rules & Rates Decoded** — Neutral, fast explainers on Fed policy, IRS changes, and regulatory shifts.
5. **Myth vs Reality** — Unpacking viral money myths, tax misconceptions, and common traps.
6. **Saturday Deep Dives** — 10-minute long-form documentaries dissecting US monetary structures.

## 2. Initial 15 Video Production Queue

### 1. Where Does $100 of Your Paycheck Go? (Short #1)
- **Format**: Short (9:16, 42s) | **Style**: Isometric Infographic
- **Hook**: "You just earned $100 on your paycheck. But before it touches your bank account, where did $24 vanish?"
- **Core Breakdown**: Gross $100 → $6.20 Social Security, $1.45 Medicare, $10.50 Federal Income Tax, $4.50 State Tax → $77.35 Net Pay.
- **Key Insight**: How mandatory FICA differs from progressive income tax.

### 2. Where Does $100 of Gas Money Go? (Short #2)
- **Format**: Short (9:16, 38s) | **Style**: Data Storytelling
- **Hook**: "You just pumped $100 into your tank. How much did the gas station actually keep?"
- **Core Breakdown**: $52 Crude oil, $19 Refining cost, $15 State & Federal excise taxes, $14 Distribution & station margin (often < $2 profit per fill-up).
- **Key Insight**: Gas stations make their profit on snacks and coffee, not the gasoline.

### 3. Deductible vs. Out-of-Pocket Max in 45 Seconds (Short #3)
- **Format**: Short (9:16, 44s) | **Style**: Swiss Motion Graphics
- **Hook**: "Most people think hitting their deductible means free medical care. That mistake can cost you thousands."
- **Core Breakdown**: Deductible is where insurance *starts* paying a percentage (coinsurance); Out-of-Pocket Max is where they pay 100%.
- **Key Insight**: Visualizing the 3 stages: 100% You → Coinsurance Split → 100% Insurer.

### 4. Where Does Your Health Insurance Premium Go? (Short #4)
- **Format**: Short (9:16, 40s) | **Style**: Pictogram Motion
- **Hook**: "Federal law legally forces health insurers to spend 80 to 85 cents of every dollar you pay them on one thing."
- **Core Breakdown**: Medical Loss Ratio (80/20 rule). $80-$85 on hospital/doctor claims; $15-$20 on admin, customer service, and profit.
- **Key Insight**: What triggers annual rebate checks in the mail.

### 5. Why Your Car Insurance Spiked (Even With Zero Accidents) (Short #5)
- **Format**: Short (9:16, 45s) | **Style**: Chalkboard Explainer
- **Hook**: "Perfect driving record, no tickets, no claims—yet your auto insurance renewal jumped 18%."
- **Core Breakdown**: Insurance is a shared risk pool. Modern car repair costs (+34% sensor bumpers), severe weather loss spikes, and parts inflation.
- **Key Insight**: You aren't just paying for your risk; you pay for the repair costs of everyone in your ZIP code.

### 6. How a Health Insurance Claim Moves (Step-by-Step) (Short #6)
- **Format**: Short (9:16, 48s) | **Style**: Transit Map / Blueprint
- **Hook**: "The bizarre journey a medical bill takes after you walk out of the doctor's office."
- **Core Breakdown**: Charge submitted → Allowed amount negotiated → Deductible applied → Coinsurance split → Explanation of Benefits (EOB).
- **Key Insight**: An EOB is never a bill; why you should always wait for the matching provider invoice.

### 7. The Mortgage Amortization Trap (Short #7)
- **Format**: Short (9:16, 42s) | **Style**: Data Physicalization
- **Hook**: "On your first $2,000 mortgage payment, how much of the house do you actually own?"
- **Core Breakdown**: Month 1 breakdown of a $350k loan at 6.8%: ~$1,980 in interest, ~$300 towards principal.
- **Key Insight**: Why the first 10 years feel like renting from the bank, and how one extra payment impacts the curve.

### 8. What Actually Makes Up a FICO Credit Score? (Short #8)
- **Format**: Short (9:16, 40s) | **Style**: Whiteboard Explainer
- **Hook**: "Closing an old credit card actually hurts your score. Here is the mathematical reason why."
- **Core Breakdown**: 35% Payment History, 30% Credit Utilization, 15% Length of History, 10% Credit Mix, 10% New Inquiries.
- **Key Insight**: Utilization has no memory, but average age of accounts does.

### 9. APR vs APY: The Hidden Compounding Difference (Short #9)
- **Format**: Short (9:16, 36s) | **Style**: Dark Tech Keynote
- **Hook**: "Banks use APR when you owe them money, but APY when they pay you. Here is why."
- **Core Breakdown**: APR is simple annual interest without compounding; APY includes compounding interest over the year.
- **Key Insight**: Always compare APY to APY for savings, and read compounding frequency.

### 10. What Inflation Does to $10,000 Over 10 Years (Short #10)
- **Format**: Short (9:16, 42s) | **Style**: Isometric Infographic
- **Hook**: "Leaving $10,000 in a traditional 0.01% checking account is guaranteed loss."
- **Core Breakdown**: At historical 3.2% inflation, $10,000 in cash has the buying power of ~$7,280 in 10 years.
- **Key Insight**: Inflation is the silent holding tax on stagnant cash.

### 11. Where Is Your Cash Really Safe? (FDIC Explained) (Short #11)
- **Format**: Short (9:16, 45s) | **Style**: Visual Investigation
- **Hook**: "If your bank fails tomorrow morning, what happens to your deposits?"
- **Core Breakdown**: $250,000 per depositor, per insured bank, per ownership category.
- **Key Insight**: Joint accounts get $500,000; fintech apps vs FDIC-insured partner banks.

### 12. What a Fed Rate Cut Actually Does to Your Wallet (Short #12)
- **Format**: Short (9:16, 42s) | **Style**: Caption News
- **Hook**: "When Jerome Powell cuts interest rates by 25 basis points, who wins and who loses?"
- **Core Breakdown**: Winners: Credit card variable rates lower, mortgage refis, auto loans. Losers: High-yield savings accounts drop yield.
- **Key Insight**: The ripple effect from overnight Fed funds to commercial prime rates.

### 13. Top 5 Reasons Insurance Claims Get Denied (Short #13)
- **Format**: Short (9:16, 48s) | **Style**: Archive Light Table
- **Hook**: "Over 16% of in-network health claims are denied on first pass. Most for stupid administrative errors."
- **Core Breakdown**: Prior authorization missing, out-of-network provider at in-network facility, coding mismatch, filing deadline passed.
- **Key Insight**: How to request an internal appeal and overturn administrative denials.

### 14. What Does FICA on Your Paycheck Actually Pay For? (Short #14)
- **Format**: Short (9:16, 40s) | **Style**: Pictogram Motion
- **Hook**: "You see 'FICA' deducted every two weeks. What does that acronym stand for and where does it go?"
- **Core Breakdown**: Federal Insurance Contributions Act. Funds Social Security old-age & disability trust funds and Medicare Hospital Insurance.
- **Key Insight**: It's a dedicated payroll trust fund, distinct from the general Treasury fund.

- **Structure**:

  - Act 4 (7:00 - 8:45): The Rising Interest on National Debt.
  - Act 5 (8:45 - 10:00): What it means for the next generation & Key Takeaway.
