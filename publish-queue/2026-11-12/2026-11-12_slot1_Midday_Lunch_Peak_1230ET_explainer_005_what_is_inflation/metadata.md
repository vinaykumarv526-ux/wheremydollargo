What is Inflation? (The Candy Bar Rule)

What actually is inflation? Inflation isn't things getting more expensive; it's your money losing its purchasing power. Imagine there are only ten candy bars on an island, and everyone has a total of ten dollars. Each candy bar costs one dollar. Now imagine a helicopter flies over and drops one hundred dollars onto the island. The number of candy bars didn't change, but now there is way more cash chasing the exact same ten candy bars.
