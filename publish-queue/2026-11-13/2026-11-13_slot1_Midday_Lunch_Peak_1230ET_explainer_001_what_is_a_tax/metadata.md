What is a Tax? (Explained in 40 Seconds)

What actually is a tax? Think of a country like a giant apartment building. Everyone needs clean water, working elevators, security, and repairs in the hallways. But no single tenant wants to pay the entire bill alone. Taxes are simply the subscription fee you pay to live in a civilized society. When you work, you pay Income Tax. When you buy a coffee, you pay Sales Tax. And when you own a house, you pay Property Tax. That pooled money funds everything from public roads and firefighters to schools and national defense.
