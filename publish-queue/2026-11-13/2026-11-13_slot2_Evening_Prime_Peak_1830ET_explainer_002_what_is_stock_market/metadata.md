What is the Stock Market? (The Pizza Analogy)

What actually is the stock market? Imagine your friend starts a pizza shop. Business is booming, and they want to open ten more shops, but they need one million dollars. Instead of taking a giant bank loan, they slice their business into one million tiny pieces called shares, and sell each share for one dollar. The stock market is simply the digital grocery store where people buy and sell those tiny slices of real businesses. If the pizza shop grows, each slice becomes worth five dollars.
