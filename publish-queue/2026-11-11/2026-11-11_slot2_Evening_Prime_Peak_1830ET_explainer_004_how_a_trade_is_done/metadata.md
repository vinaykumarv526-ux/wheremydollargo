How a Trade is Actually Done (Behind the Scenes)

You open Robinhood or Schwab, tap Buy, and hear a little chime. What actually happened behind your phone screen? In under one second, your broker routes your order to an electronic market maker or stock exchange like the Nasdaq. High-speed computers scan millions of sell orders to find someone selling that exact stock at your price. The moment a match is found, your cash is locked, and the shares appear in your portfolio.
