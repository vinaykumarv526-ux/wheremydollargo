What is a Trade in the Stock Market?

What actually is a financial trade? A trade is simply an agreement between two people who disagree on the future. Imagine you own one share of Apple. You think Apple has reached its peak and might drop, so you want to sell it for two hundred dollars. Somewhere across the world, a stranger believes Apple is about to release a revolutionary product and will climb to three hundred dollars, so they want to buy it for two hundred dollars.
