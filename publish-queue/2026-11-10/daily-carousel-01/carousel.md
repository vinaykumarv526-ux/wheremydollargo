### 📌 Slide 2: Interest Rates & Mortgage Watch
- **Headline**: **Average 30-Year Fixed Mortgage Holds at 6.62%**
- **The Number**: `6.62%` (down 4 bps week-over-week)
- **The Driver**: Bond market stabilizing following Treasury auction demand.
- **What it means for you**: On a \$400,000 mortgage loan, a 0.25% dip in rate saves roughly \$65 per month in interest payment—or nearly \$23,400 over a 30-year amortization.
- **Primary Source**: Freddie Mac Primary Mortgage Market Survey (PMMS).

---

### 📌 Slide 3: Banking & High-Yield Savings
- **Headline**: **Top High-Yield Savings Accounts (HYSA) Average 4.45% APY**
- **The Number**: `4.45% APY` vs `0.07%` Traditional Checking/Savings
- **The Context**: Commercial banks maintain competitive deposit yields while Fed rates remain steady.
- **What it means for you**: Parking a \$10,000 emergency fund in an HYSA yields ~\$445 a year in passive compound interest, compared to less than \$7 at traditional legacy big-box banks.
- **Primary Source**: FDIC National Rates & Deposit Benchmarks.

---

### 📌 Slide 4: Retail Energy & Pump Prices
- **Headline**: **National Average Gasoline Settles at $3.12 / Gallon**
- **The Number**: `$3.12 / gal`
- **The Breakdown**: Crude oil accounts for 52% of the price; refining, transport, and federal/state fuel excise taxes make up the remaining 48%.
- **What it means for you**: A typical 14-gallon vehicle fill-up costs \$43.68, with roughly \$6.55 going to state and federal road maintenance trust funds.
- **Primary Source**: U.S. Energy Information Administration (EIA) Weekly Retail Gasoline Report.
